### Description [2878/4000]
Do you play in a Baroque ensemble at A=415? Sing in a choir tuned to 442? Practise with recordings that are never quite at your A? RePitch brings every track to your instrument’s pitch, in real time and without touching the tempo.

SET YOUR A, ONCE
Choose the reference A from 392 to 465 Hz, one hertz at a time, or start from the presets: 415 (Baroque), 430 (Classical), 432, 440, 442, 443, 392 (French Baroque), 465 (Venetian). It applies to every track. You can also transpose by semitones and fine-tune in cents.

EACH TRACK’S A, DETECTED FOR YOU
Every recording has a tuning of its own. RePitch analyses your tracks on the device, detects their A and works out the difference from your instrument by itself. When an estimate isn’t reliable it tells you (“uncertain”) and doesn’t apply it: you can always set the value by hand.

OWN TUNING, TRACK BY TRACK
Does one piece need something different? Open it in the player and give it its own semitones and cents. The others stay on the global tuning, and you find that track just as you left it next time.

A METRONOME THAT FOLLOWS THE TRACK
The click and a flash on the screen follow the beats of the track you are playing, even when the music slows down or speeds up. The beats are found on your device by an open-source neural network (Beat This!, MIT licence): the first analysis of a track takes a few seconds and is then remembered. If the result is off, you can correct it by tapping along, or halve or double the tempo, and choose how many beats make a bar, with the accent on the “1”.

BUILT FOR PRACTICE AND REHEARSAL
• Searchable library: import your own audio files from the Files app
• Playlists for lessons, rehearsals and concerts: each track keeps its own tuning
• Playback continues with the screen locked
• Accurate metronome, generated inside the audio engine: at a fixed tempo or following the track, with the screen kept on
• Chromatic tuner with a big, colour-coded needle (green in tune, red when far off), readable from a distance on a music stand
• Historical temperaments in the tuner: equal, Pythagorean, meantone, Werckmeister, Kirnberger, Vallotti, Young, just intonation, or one entirely your own; the reference tone follows the temperament and your A
• Works on iPhone and iPad, in English and Italian
• Settings backup to iCloud Drive or wherever you like, to find them again on a new phone

SUPPORTED FORMATS
MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, CAF and FLAC. DRM-protected tracks, such as those from Apple Music, can’t be imported.

RESPECT FOR YOU AND YOUR MUSIC
• Free, no ads
• No account
• Works offline
• Collects no data: your tracks stay on your device
• The microphone is used only by the tuner, only while it is open, and the sound is never recorded

RePitch is made by one person, for musicians who practise with recordings. Questions or suggestions? Write to us from the About page in the app.

### App Review notes (English) [1574/4000]
Hello, and thank you for reviewing RePitch.

RePitch plays the user's own audio files at a different pitch (semitones and cents, plus a global reference A such as 440 or 442) and includes a metronome and a chromatic tuner. There is no login or account, no in-app purchases, no ads and no data collection; everything stays on the device. The app works offline.

Microphone: the microphone is used ONLY by the Tuner tab, to recognise the pitch of the note the user plays. It is active only while the Tuner tab is open. The audio is analysed in real time on the device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. If access is denied, every other feature keeps working. The permission text is: "RePitch uses the microphone only for the tuner, to recognise the note you play."

Background audio: the app declares the audio background mode only so that playback of the user's own tracks continues with the screen locked.

How to test (no login needed):
1. Open the Library tab and tap "Import". Pick any audio file from the Files app (MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, CAF or FLAC). Any short song or sample will do.
2. Tap the imported track to play it. The track's A is detected automatically after import.
3. Open the player (tap the mini player at the bottom) and change the semitones or cents: the pitch changes while the tempo stays the same.
4. Open the Tuning tab to change the global reference A (for example 442).
5. Open the Tuner tab and allow microphone access when asked; play or sing a note, or tap the reference note, to see the tuner react.
