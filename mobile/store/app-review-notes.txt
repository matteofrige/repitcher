2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
RePitch is a practice tool for musicians (students, amateurs, choirs, orchestral and early-music players) who play along with recordings. Instruments are often not tuned to A = 440 Hz (e.g. 442 Hz orchestras, 415/432 Hz baroque ensembles, an old piano), and recordings use different tunings too, so playing along sounds out of tune. RePitch plays the user's own audio files re-pitched to the user's instrument, in real time, without changing the tempo:
- Tuning tab: set your instrument's A (Hz), plus a global transposition in semitones and cents.
- Library: import audio files; RePitch detects each track's reference A on the device and aligns it to yours.
- Player: per-track override (semitones/cents), saved per track; playback continues with the screen locked.
- Playlists: create, rename, delete, reorder (drag & drop).
- Metronome and Tuner (the tuner uses the microphone, processed only on the device, never recorded or sent). The tuner offers historical temperaments (Werckmeister, Vallotti, meantone…).
- Metronome on the track (new in 1.1.0): in the Player, a click and flash that follow the beats of the playing track. The beats are found on the device by a small open-source neural network (Beat This!, MIT licence) run with Core ML; nothing is uploaded.

3. HOW TO TEST
No login or setup needed. Sample files (synthesised by us, public-domain melody): https://re-pitch.it/samples/repitch-samples.zip
- In Safari on the device open the link and download it; in Files tap the zip to extract it.
- Open RePitch, skip/finish the short guide, go to Library > Import and pick minuet-a440.m4a and minuet-a432.m4a. The library shows "Track’s A 440 Hz · detected" and "Track’s A 432 Hz · detected".
- Tuning tab: set "Your instrument’s A" (e.g. 442 Hz) and transposition; tap a track to play it at that tuning.
- In the Player, change semitones/cents for that track only.
- Playlists tab: create a playlist, add both tracks, tap Edit to reorder by dragging.
- In the Player, turn on "Metronome on the track": after a short on-device analysis the click follows the music.
- Metronome and Tuner tabs: tap Start (the tuner asks for microphone permission); in the Tuner, tap Temperament to choose one.
Individual files are also at https://re-pitch.it/samples/minuet-a440.m4a and https://re-pitch.it/samples/minuet-a432.m4a

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. No backend, no analytics, no ads, no AI services, no payment processor, no third-party SDKs that collect data. All audio processing happens on the device (open-source Signalsmith Stretch library and the Beat This! beat-tracking model, both MIT licence). The only network use is opening our website (privacy policy) and the App Store rating sheet; feedback opens the Mail app.
